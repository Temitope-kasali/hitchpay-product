## Product Philosophy

My approach to fintech product development is grounded in three principles:

### Build for real financial workflows

Products should solve meaningful problems across how businesses hold, move, manage and monitor money.

### Make complexity operationally simple

Financial infrastructure can be technically and regulatorily complex. The product experience should make that complexity understandable and actionable for customers and internal teams.

### Use AI where it improves decisions

AI should augment product and operational decision-making rather than obscure responsibility.

For financial risk products in particular, intelligent systems need appropriate controls, evidence, explainability and human oversight.
